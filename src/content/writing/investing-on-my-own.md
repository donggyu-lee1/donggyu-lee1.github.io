I have been researching investments on my own, from simple crypto rules to event studies, relative-value trades, and the software around them. This series includes the experiments that failed and the work that stopped at missing data.

The early articles follow the starting process; later ones are grouped by strategy. The final three discuss research, backtesting, and operations. Evidence is reviewed through September 22, 2026. These are research records, not investment recommendations or promises of future returns. Simulated amounts are not my actual account balances.
